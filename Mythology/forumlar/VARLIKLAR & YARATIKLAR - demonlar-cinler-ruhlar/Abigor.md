# Abigor
### Grand Duke of Hades, Demon of the Superior Order
> **Rütbe:** Grand Duke · **Köken:** Goetia, Dictionnaire Infernal, Pseudomonarchia Daemonum · **Lejyon:** 60 · **Abilities:** Knows secrets of war, sees the future, foretells events, teaches leaders how to win loyalty of soldiers, instructs leaders on making themselves respected by soldiers · **Kaynak:** Guiley, Rosemary Ellen - The Encyclopedia of Demons and Demonology; The Demon Lists - Compiled by Rev. Satrinah Nagash; Bane, Theresa - Encyclopedia of demons in world religions and cultures · **Tür:** demon
*A grand duke of Hell who appears as a handsome knight on horseback, possessing knowledge of war secrets and prophecy, commanding 60 legions of demons.*

Abigor appears as a handsome man on a horse, holding a standard or scepter. He knows all the secrets of war and sees the future. He teaches leaders how to win the loyalty of soldiers. In hell, he commands 60 legions of demons. He is shown in the form of a handsome knight bearing a lance, standard or scepter. He is a demon of the superior order and responds readily to questions of war. He can foretell the future and instructs leaders on how to make themselves respected by soldiers. Also called Abigar. In Johann Wierus's Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, Abigor is listed as one of the twelve servitors of Abezethibou, ranked as a duke.

### Hüküm Alanları
- War
- prophecy
- military leadership
- soldier loyalty
