# Amon
### Grand Marquis of Hell, Strongest of the Princes of the Demons
> **Rütbe:** Marquis · **Köken:** Goetia, Christian demonology, demonized form of Egyptian god Amun · **Personality:** Strong and powerful; willing to obey the conjurer's command to change form; capable of reconciliation and procuring love; associated with prophecy and knowledge of temporal events · **Lejyon:** 40 · **Tür:** demon
*A powerful marquis of Hell who knows past and future, reconciles friends and enemies, and procures love; one of the seventy-two Spirits of Solomon with probable origins as the demonized Egyptian deity Amun.*

![gorsel](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/mythology/images/f/f0/Amon.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/774?cb=20250617221401)

Amon is the seventh spirit mentioned in the Goetia and one of the seventy-two Spirits of Solomon. He is described as a strong and powerful marquis of the infernal empire who commands 40 legions of demons. He appears first as a wolf with a serpent's tail, vomiting flame. When commanded by the conjurer, he assumes human form but with the head of a large owl possessing canine teeth (some sources say dog's teeth or raven's head with dog's teeth). By the time Collin de Plancy wrote his Dictionnaire Infernal in 1818, all traces of the Egyptian origin had been lost; in the 1863 edition, de Plancy describes the demon as having the body of a wolf and the tail of a serpent, and if persuaded to appear in human form, he will emit fire from his mouth. Amon is summoned for his knowledge of the past and his ability to predict the future. He has the power to make men and women fall in love with each other and can settle disputes between friends and enemies, reconciling those who have quarreled when he wills it. He is also described as the strongest of all demons and the strongest of the princes of the demons. He is in direct service under Satanachia. The demon king called Maymon (Mammon) is likely also derived from this image of Amon. In Hebrew, his name means 'lion of God.' He is associated with the Egyptian god Amun, indicated by his appearance with the head of a huge bird or owl, suggesting a demonized form of that deity. He teaches the secrets of love and gives knowledge of past and future events.

### Tezahür
First appears as a wolf with a serpent's tail, vomiting flame; when assuming human form, has the head of a large owl with canine teeth (or raven's head with dog's teeth); alternatively described as having the body of a wolf and tail of a serpent, emitting fire from mouth in human form; de Plancy 1863 edition describes body of wolf with tail of serpent
