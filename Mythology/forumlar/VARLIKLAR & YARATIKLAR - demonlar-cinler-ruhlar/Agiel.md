# Agiel
### Intelligency of Saturn
> **Rütbe:** Intelligency · **Köken:** Medieval tradition, Kabbalistic/occult demonology · **Sigil:** The sigil given under the name Agiel for letter A in the secret 'Alphabet of Angels or the Writing and Language of Heaven'; widely copied in demonic literature · **Kaynak:** Dictionary of Demons, Fred Gettings, 1988; Encyclopedia of Demons in World Religions and Cultures, Theresa Bane · **Tür:** demon
*Agiel is the intelligency of Saturn, associated with the daemon Zazel and the magical number 45 in medieval occult tradition. The sigil given under the name Agiel for letter A in the secret 'Alphabet of Angels or the Writing and Language of Heaven' has been widely copied in demonic literature.*

In the medieval tradition, Agiel is ascribed the magical number 45 and is associated with the daemon ZAZEL (see MAGIC SQUARES). The sigil given under the name Agiel for letter A in the secret 'Alphabet of Angels or the Writing and Language of Heaven' (see ALPHABET OF DEMONS) has been widely copied in the demonic literature. See INTELLIGENCIES. According to Agrippa's list of planetary intelligencies and corresponding demons, Agiel is the Intelligency of Saturn, paired with the Daemonium Zazel. The Intelligencies were originally the high spirits who governed and regulated the movement of the planetary and celestial spheres (later called Motori or Movitori), though in later literature they were regarded as planetary spirits concerned with the planet which was the hub of the particular celestial sphere over which each had rule. Agrippa's list appears to have entered the post-medieval demonic tradition. In the Enochian sequence of the Alphabet of Demons, Agiel appears as the first name corresponding to the letter A.
