# Andras
### Grand Marquis of Hell, Demon of Quarrels
> **Rütbe:** Marquis · **Köken:** Christian demonology, Goetia, Lesser Key of Solomon · **Appearance:** Appears as a naked man with angelic wings and the head of an owl or black night-raven; rides a strong black wolf; carries a gleaming sharp sword or saber in his hands · **Personality:** Has little patience; extremely dangerous; will kill anyone who gives the slightest provocation; kills those not constantly aware of his presence; causes discord and convinces men to kill · **Lejyon:** 30 · **Abilities:** Sows discord; convinces men to kill; teaches how to kill enemies; kills the unwary · **Tür:** demon
*Fallen angel and 63rd of the 72 Spirits of Solomon, a dangerous marquis who sows discord and kills the unwary.*

Andras is a fallen angel and great marquis in Hell who rules over 30 legions of demons, though conflicting sources claim him to be a prince. When summoned, he appears as a naked man with a set of angelic wings and the head of an owl or black night-raven. He rides upon the back of a strong black wolf and wields a gleaming sharp saber or sword. Andras has little patience and will kill anyone who gives him the slightest provocation, especially those who are not constantly aware of his presence when he is around. He causes discord and has the ability to convince men to kill. He teaches people how to kill their enemies. The demon is considered extremely dangerous, and if the magician is not careful, he will be slain. Andras creates discord and kills those who are not careful and wary, including the master, servants, and all assistants of any household.
