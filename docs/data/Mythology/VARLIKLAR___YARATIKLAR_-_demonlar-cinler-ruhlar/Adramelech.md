# Adramelech

*Originally a Samarian sun god worshipped at Sepharavim where children were burned alive as sacrifices, later demonized in Judeo-Christian tradition as a high-ranking chancellor of Hell and one of the ten evil sephiroths, now serving under Samael as the evil counterpart to the divine Sephiroth Hod.*

![gorsel](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0d/Dictionnaire_infernal_-_Adrammelech.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail_unscaled)

Adramelech was originally a Samarian deity worshipped in Assyria at the town of Sepharavim, where children were sacrificed to him by being burned alive upon his altars. In Syria, he was known as 'Wing King' (Adramelech) and was worshipped by the Sepharvites. His name was later demonized in Judeo-Christian tradition, and he was placed in service under Samael, the angel of poison. He holds numerous high-ranking positions in the hierarchy of Hell, including Chancellor of Infernal Regions, Chancellor of the Grand Cross Order of the Fly, Commander of Hell, King of Fire, President of the Council, Prince of Fire, and Superintendent of the Wardrobe (also described as Supervisor of Satan's Wardrobe, Keeper of the wardrobe of the Demon King, and President of the Senate of the Demons). He is one of the ten evil sephiroths, specifically the eighth, making him the evil counterpart or negation of the eighth divine Sephiroth Hod. He is also described as the patron demon of hypocrisy. When summoned, he appears as a mule or peacock. The rabbis say he comes in the form of a mule and sometimes a peacock. He is one of the Fallen Angels, formerly of the Order of Archangels, and is technically an Archdemon or Djinn of Samael. His personal adversaries are the angels Uriel and Raphael. He has many similarities to the demon Adramaleck (also spelled Adramalek, Adramelec, Adar-malik, Adramaleck, Adramelek, Adrammelech). In the Infernal Empire, he serves as the president of the Supreme Council.
