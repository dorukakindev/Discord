# Anamelech
### Bearer of Ill News
> **Köken:** Assyrian demonology; Sepharvaun worship · **Appearance:** Reveals himself in the figure of a quail · **Personality:** Bearer of ill news, obscure demon associated with bringing bad tidings · **Element:** Moon (according to some sources, as counterpart to Andramelech as the sun) · **Kaynak:** The Demon Lists - Compiled by Rev. Satrinah Nagash; Kimberly Daniels - The Demon Dictionary Volume One · **Tür:** demon
*An obscure Assyrian demon worshipped at Sepharvaun, known as the harbinger of bad tidings who reveals himself in the form of a quail.*

Anamelech is an obscure demon known as the bearer of ill news. He was worshipped at Sepharvaun, an Assyrian town. He always reveals himself in the figure of a quail. His name, as some believe, signifies a 'good king', and some also say this demon is the moon, as Andramelech is the sun. According to Kimberly Daniels' The Demon Dictionary, Anamelech is considered the harbinger of bad tidings.
