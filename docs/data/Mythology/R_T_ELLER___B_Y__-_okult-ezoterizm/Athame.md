*The Witch's traditional magical tool, a consecrated black-handled dagger used in ritual work for drawing magic circles, invoking, and directing energy, never used for physical cutting.*

The Athame is the Witch's traditional magical tool, pronounced "a-THAY-me". It is a consecrated, black-handled dagger that is never used to cut on the material plane, but can be used to defend. It is a personal knife, usually made of steel and is double-bladed or double-edged. The double edge symbolizes that power works both ways: healing and harming, creation and destruction, light and dark—both sides are necessary and both sides need to be controlled. The black handle absorbs power. The owner often engraves magick symbols into the metal. As a magical tool associated with change, it is the symbol of the Element Fire, and its phallic nature links it to the God. Its direction is the South. According to another source, the Athame is a cleansed and consecrated ceremonial knife used in ritual work by Witches, rarely used to cut anything on the material plane.
